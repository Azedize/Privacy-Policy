Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Chrome extension ("Extension") collects, uses, stores, and protects information. It is intended to be clear, concise, and transparent.

Information Collection

The Extension does not collect, store, sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Any data accessed by the Extension is used solely to provide the functionality requested by the user.

Host Permissions

The Extension requests host permissions only for the websites that the user chooses to interact with. These permissions enable the Extension to perform automation tasks, read page content, and interact with web elements as intended.

Debugger Permission

The Extension may require debugger permissions to interact with browser debugging APIs. This capability supports advanced automation and diagnostic features.

Debugger access is used exclusively for the Extension's intended operation and is not used to collect personal information.

Data Storage

User preferences and settings are stored locally in the browser unless otherwise disclosed. The Extension does not transfer this data to external servers unless the user explicitly initiates such transfer.

Third-Party Services

The Extension does not sell user data and does not share personal information with third-party advertisers or marketing services.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are implemented to protect user information and ensure the secure operation of the Extension.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be published on this page with the revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Extension developer through the support information provided with the Extension.

This document reflects the current Privacy Policy as of the effective date.
